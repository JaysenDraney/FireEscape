<div class="fe-class-grid">

| # | Class | What it does |
|---|-------|--------------|
| 1 | Theme & layout | Colors, fonts, row arrangements. Data only, no code. |
| 2 | Wallpaper | Background images or video behind the home screen. |
| 3 | Widgets | Small live info in header slots (clock, weather). |
| 4 | Idle | Full-screen content while the TV is idle. |
| 5 | Content rows | Rows of cards that open something when selected. |
| 6 | Launch sources | Apps, inputs, links — things you can open. |
| 7 | Catalogs | Titles and metadata for browsing and search. |
| 8 | Availability | Where a title can be watched. |
| 9 | Recommendations | Ranked suggestions with reasons. |
| 10 | Automation | Event-triggered actions (open app at boot). |
| 11 | Profiles | Per-profile visibility and restrictions. |

</div>
